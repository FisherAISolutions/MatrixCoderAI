# Legal Review Required

The in-app Terms, Privacy, Acceptable Use, AI Content, Support, and Data Request pages are private-beta implementation placeholders. They are not legal advice and must not be represented as professionally approved.

Before public launch, qualified counsel should review:

- service eligibility, ownership, licenses, warranties, limitations, termination, and disputes;
- data categories, subprocessors, retention, deletion, transfers, and user rights;
- essential browser storage, telemetry, analytics, and any future marketing cookies;
- acceptable AI use, generated-content disclosures, abuse reporting, and prohibited content;
- payment, renewal, cancellation, refund, tax, and grace-period language;
- support commitments and incident communications.

Every newly enabled analytics, advertising, storage, AI, billing, or deployment provider must trigger a disclosure review.
